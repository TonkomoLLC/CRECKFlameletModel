OF13 patch note:
- Removed TypeName("pdfFlameletThermo") from the templated implementation class.
  The final concrete thermo type is registered by makeThermo/NamedThermo in flameletThermos.C.
  Leaving TypeName in the template declares per-instantiation static typeName/debug members
  that are not defined, causing linker errors when flameletSimpleFoam links against
  libflameletThermophysicalModels.so.
